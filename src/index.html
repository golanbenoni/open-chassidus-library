The library standard 02 / 03

Open by design.
Faithful by practice.

The library is built around source fidelity and public access. Automation may help people find a passage; it never becomes the source.

01

Free to read

Reading and downloads will not require accounts or payment.

↗
02

Complete editions

Released works will be complete, edition-aware, and identified by stable references.

↗
03

Source on every record

Edition, year, source, rights basis, transcription history, and checksum travel with the text.

↗
04

AI does not author

The canonical corpus stays faithful to documented editions. Search tools point back to the text.

↗
05

Changes are visible

Corrections and edition changes are reviewed, dated, and preserved in version history.

↗
06

Built to outlast one host

Versioned downloads and mirrors will make the collection independently preservable.

↗

Each edition is identified separately, with its source, version title, license metadata, rights review, and checksum. The corpus does not treat one edition's rights as applying to every edition of a work.
